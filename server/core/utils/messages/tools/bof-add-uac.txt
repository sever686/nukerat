关于
    Add UserAccountControl flag. Supports standard LDAP (389) with
    signing/sealing and LDAPS (636).
用法：
    bof-add-uac --- {target} {flag} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]

示例：
    bof-add-uac --- {target} {flag} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]

NUKERAT 选项：
    -w <秒数>       等待超时的最大时间
    -bg             后台运行，不等待输出

    示例：
    bof-add-uac -w 60 --- {target} {flag} [-ou {path}] [-dc {ip}] [--ldaps]
