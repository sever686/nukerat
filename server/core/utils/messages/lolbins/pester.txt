    Used as part of the Powershell pester. LOLBAS documents proxy
    execution.

用法：
    Pester.bat [/help|?|-?|/?] "$null; {CMD}"
    Pester.bat ;{PATH:.exe}

示例：
    Pester.bat [/help|?|-?|/?] "$null; {CMD}"
    Pester.bat ;{PATH:.exe}

NUKERAT 选项：
    -w <秒数>       等待超时的最大时间
    -bg             后台运行，不等待输出

    示例：
    Pester.bat [/help|?|-?|/?] "$null; {CMD}"
